# DT610 proof-of-delivery camera demo

Use a fictitious shipping label. Do not put a real personal address, phone number, or parcel number in the shot.

Record with a second phone so the reviewer can see the DT610 and the screen. A screen recording made on the DT610 itself can change the capture time. If one is used during a timed test, say so in the evidence.

1. **0–15 seconds.** Show the DT610, open **POD camera test**, and show the rear camera ID, autofocus modes, and the selected JPEG size. Say that a JPEG pixel count is not the sensor specification. The hardware 13 MP claim stays **SPEC CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** until an authoritative specification is confirmed in the app.
2. **15–40 seconds.** Put the mock label under normal light. Tap **CAPTURE & MEASURE** once. Show the capture-to-saved time, the measured width and height, and the file size in bytes. Tap **VIEW IMAGE**, zoom in, and look at the address, tracking number, and barcode. Then set **LEGIBILITY PASS** or **LEGIBILITY FAIL** from that visual check.
3. **40–70 seconds.** Tap **RUN 30 CAPTURES**, or cut to a finished run and say it is an automated repeat, not 30 separate button presses. Show the saved count, the maximum time, and how many captures exceeded 3000 ms.
4. **70–90 seconds.** Tap **EXPORT CSV** and **EXPORT JSON**, then **SHARE SAMPLE IMAGE**. Show the files. Keep the product specification as a separate attachment.

The overall result is **PASS** only when the rear camera reports still-picture autofocus, a saved JPEG is at least 13,000,000 pixels, the operator has confirmed the product specification, legibility is **PASS**, and every measured capture saved in 3000 ms or less.
